# Backgammon Legacy v1.11.0 — Slim Premium HUD

Visual-only gameplay composition update based on the v1.10 in-game review.

## Changes
- Reduced top HUD from 42 to 30 logical units.
- Reduced bottom control deck from 56 to 39 logical units.
- Increased live board height from 178 to 207 logical units (+16.3%).
- Kept the 510 × 280 composition and centred safe-window scaling.
- Reauthored Classic Burgundy player panels, turn plaque, bottom deck and button state artwork with thinner antique-gold trim and subtler leather/bevel treatment.
- Reduced avatar, score, menu/settings and button typography sizes to fit the slimmer chrome without crowding.
- Reduced dice from 38 to 27 logical units and placed them in recessed wells.
- Made Undo and Hint compact single-line controls.
- Preserved pressed/disabled button states.
- Added `design/reference/V1_11_RUNTIME_COMPOSITION.png` as the runtime proportion reference.

## Unchanged
- Backgammon, Mahbouseh and Tawla 31 rules.
- AI.
- XP / Gold / reward system.
- BoardMap source geometry and touch/animation anchors.
- Checker sizing rule and checker cosmetics.
- Right-side bear-off behaviour.
